# Pairs for meaning check

Each pair: an ORIGINAL sentence from a published article and a LIGHT (plainer) rewrite.

## Pair ¶1

ORIGINAL: They pick up worn-out notebooks, walk to overcrowded learning centers with very limited resources, or teach themselves because there is nowhere else for them to continue their education.

LIGHT: They pick up worn-out notebooks and walk to overcrowded learning centers with very limited resources. Or they teach themselves, because there is nowhere else for them to continue their education.

## Pair ¶2

ORIGINAL: Rohingya refugees are not allowed to pursue formal education in Bangladesh because of government policies that restrict their access to the national education system.

LIGHT: Rohingya refugees are not allowed to pursue formal education in Bangladesh, because government policies restrict their access to the national education system.

## Pair ¶3

ORIGINAL: What they have lost is something very different — the opportunity to learn freely, continue their studies, and build the future they imagined before violence forced them to flee their homeland.

LIGHT: What they have lost is something very different. It is the opportunity to learn freely, continue their studies, and build the future they imagined before violence forced them to flee their homeland.
